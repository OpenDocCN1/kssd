
通过`1F-1=1E`两次的累加，即`1+2+3+4....+1D+1E=1D1`，则原始注册码+`1D1*2`（即十进制465）为正确注册码。

用`VB`写个注册机，`Text1`为机器码，`Text2`为注册码

------------------------

```vb
Dim ESI As Long

ESI = Int(Val(Text1.Text) / 7)

For i = 1 To Int(Val(Text1.Text) / 1228013) - 1
   ESI = ESI + i * 2
Next i

Text2.Text = ESI
```
