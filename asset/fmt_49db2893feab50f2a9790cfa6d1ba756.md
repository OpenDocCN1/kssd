
# Flash ActionScript Tool

> 原文：[`www.pediy.com/kssd/pediy03/forum911.htm`](https://www.pediy.com/kssd/pediy03/forum911.htm)

作者：南木

## 软件信息
- **软件名称**：Flash ActionScript Tool (FAST!), v1.0
- **软件功能**：FAST! will aide you in creating, editing, organizing and exchanging your ActionScripts easier & faster. (flash 的脚本编辑利器！！！)
- **软件限制**：If you didn't register FAST! you'll be seeing a nag-screen everytime you start the program. You also won't be able to copy more then 5 lines to the clipboard.
- **软件价格**：Registering FAST! is $39.95 US Dollars.
- **下载地址**：`http://swifftools.com/stools/`
- **使用工具**：`FI`，`W32DASM`，`trw2000`
