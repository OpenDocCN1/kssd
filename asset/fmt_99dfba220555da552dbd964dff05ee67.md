Software: `Winimage6.00.6000`

Downloaded from: `http://www.winimag.com`

Author: 看雪学苑学员 `wlq`

Tool: `trw2000`

Procedure:

## Step 1
Run `trw2000`, then hide it.

## Step 2
Run `Winimage6.0`.

## Step 3
Click "Options"-"Registering", fill in the blanks:

name: `wlq`
Registration code: `000000`

## Step 4
`Ctrl+n`, call `trw2000`, `bpx hmemcpy`, then press `F5`.

## Step 5
Click "OK", we stop at:

```
:9E62 PUSH BP
:9E63 MOV BP, SP
```

## Step 6
`BD *`
