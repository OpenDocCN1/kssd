## 变量定义与处理逻辑

```
VAR

NAME,SN,C:STRING;

LENNAME,A,B:INTEGER;

BEGIN

NAME:=EDIT1.TEXT;

LENNAME:=LENGTH(NAME);

FOR A:=1 TO LENNAME DO

BEGIN

  B:=LENNAME;

  B:=(B\*B)-(A\*A)+1;

  C:=C+INTTOSTR(B+ORD(NAME\[A\]));

END;

C:=C+EDIT2.TEXT;//连接机器码生成计算用的新字符串
```
