## Subroutine: SearchZero

```assembly
SearchZero Proc Source:DWORD
    Push Ecx
    Push Edi
    Mov Ecx, 0FFFFFFFFH
    Mov Edi, Source
    Xor Al, Al
    Repe Scasb
    Not Ecx
    Dec Ecx
    Mov Eax, Ecx
    Pop Edi
    Pop Ecx
    Ret
SearchZero EndP
```
