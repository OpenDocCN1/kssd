【算法总结】
注册码形式为XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX,取注册码的第3组第4组与"Easy Gang"的标准MD5值中的的10个字符、注册码的第1组第3位相连字符串的MD5值的5个字符，与注册码的第1组第4位和注册码的第2组相连字符串比较，若相等则注册成功

【算法注册机】
〖VB代码〗
Private Sub Command1\_Click()
   a1 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a2 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a3 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a9 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a10 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a11 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a12 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a13 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a14 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a15 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   a16 = Int(Rnd() \* 10)
   b3 = a9 & a10 & a11 & a12
   b4 = a13 & a14 & a15 & a16
   c1 = LCase(MD5("Easy Gang"))    'MD5模块
   c2 = Mid(c1, a1 + 1, 10)
   c3 = LCase(MD5(b3 & b4 & c2 & a3))
   c4 = Mid(c3, a2 + 1, 5)
   b2 = Mid(c4, 2, 4)
   a4 = Left(c4, 1)
   b1 = a1 & a2 & a3 & a4
   Text1.Text = b1 & "-" & b2 & "-" & b3 & "-" & b4
End Sub
