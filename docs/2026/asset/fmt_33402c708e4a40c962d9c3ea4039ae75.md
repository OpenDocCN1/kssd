### Blockchain Tutorial “A Gentle Introduction To Blockchain Technology” by Antony Lewis

区块链教程“BlockChain技术入门”，安东尼·刘易斯(AntonyLewis)著

![](https://bbs.kanxue.com/upload/attach/201803/790098_2AYZKJHAV89W3SY.jpg)

This modern e-book cover with particle background hides an insightful 18-pages-long paper on blockchain and Bitcoin technologies. It was assembled by the data and research company focusing on Blockchain & Digital Equities industry. They took a thorough and data-driven approach to presenting the crucial blockchain concepts. What’s more, they did a great job in terms of illustrating how does blockchain work and building comparative tables and diagrams. See a page sample below:

这个带有粒子背景的现代电子书封面隐藏了一篇长达18页的关于区块链和比特币技术的极具洞察力的论文。它是由相关数据和研究公司组装的，重点是区块链和股票金融行业。他们采取了一种全面和数据驱动的方法来呈现其中较为关键的区块链概念。更重要的是，他们在展示区块链是如何工作和建立对比图和对照表方面做的非常不错。具体详见下面的页面示例：

![](https://bbs.kanxue.com/upload/attach/201803/790098_FDT528HGS5ESGHK.jpg)

What’s inside? Let’s see. The first part of the paper is an introductory one. Firstly, it explains the main concepts and serves as a roadmap to the rest of the paper. Next, the second part explores the difference between private and public blockchains and explains the way the Bitcoin Blockchain ecosystem exists. Thirdly, you’ll find a blockchain database tutorial, which explains the structure and the forces that bring a blockchain database into being. This part dwells on the structure of the database and the ways the blockchain communicates data. It also covers such insightful topics as the ways the blockchain resolves conflicts and the ways to change the rules the blockchain follows. Finally, comes the basic explanation of how you can turn your PC into a Bitcoin blockchain node by downloading the Bitcoin core at `GitHub`.

那么其具体内容是什么呢？让我简单介绍一下:论文的第一部分是导读部分。首先，对本文的主要概念进行了阐述，并作为全文的一个学习路线图。其次，第二部分探讨了比特币区块链与共享区块链的区别，并解释了比特币区块链体制的存在方式。第三，你会发现一个区块链数据库教程，它解释了一个区块链数据库的结构和作用。这一部分详细介绍了数据库的结构和区块链通信数据的方式。除此之外，它还涵盖了一些很有讨论意义的主题，如区块链解决问题的方式以及更改区块链遵循的规则的方法。最后，还给出了如何通过下载`GitHub`的比特币核心的内容。

The ‘Gentle Introduction’ paper is fantastic if you want to start out as a Bitcoin miner and grasp the rules and principles that underpin the work of a Bitcoin database. You’ll see the schemes of the ways the chain forms the blocks, the schemes of possible hacker attacks and defense. In general terms, this tutorial invites you to take a journey that is very close to the hands-on experience.

如果你想从比特币矿商开始，掌握比特币数据库工作的规则和原则，那么“绅士介绍”这篇文章就太棒了。你会看到链子形成块的方式，黑客攻击和防御的方案。一般来说，这部教程可以带你进行一次仿佛亲身体验的旅程。
