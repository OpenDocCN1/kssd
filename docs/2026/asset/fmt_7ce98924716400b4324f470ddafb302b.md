`Type was not found or was not a compile-time constant: Vector.`

这是因为编译时，默认支持的`Flash Player`的版本是`9.0.124`。遇到这种错误时，需要修改`flex-sdk/frameworks/flex-config.xml`， 将`<target-player>9.0.124</target-player>`改为`<target-player>10.0.12</target-player>`。
